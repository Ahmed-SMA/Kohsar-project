02 / Floor program

Separate identities, one premium address.

Each floor needs its own function, access logic, and marketable identity, while the building remains one clean and controlled commercial property.

Ground

Bank public floor

Front/corner entrance, ATM, banking hall, teller counters, waiting, security, manager office, strong room, and controlled staff access.

First

Bank operations

Internal bank connection from ground floor, offices, records, meeting room, staff support, and secure operational zones.

Second

Café floor

Independent access from concealed rear/right-side lobby, café shell planning, washrooms, service, exhaust and branding frontage.

Third

Gym floor

Independent lift/stair access, ventilation, washrooms, AC outdoor zones, and clean signage without disturbing the bank.

03 / Design direction

Luxury must perform in heat.

The elevation should look premium, but its real success depends on insulation, low glass, service concealment, green shading, and long-term maintenance simplicity.

Heat-resistant shell

Insulated walls, roof insulation, minimal glass, recessed openings, light exterior materials, and controlled shading for Hyderabad heat.

Green façade strategy

Use trellis greenery, planter bands, drip irrigation, waterproofing and drainage without blocking AC outdoor airflow.

Full rooftop solar

Plan the roof as a raised solar canopy from day one, with cleaning access, wind-load design, water tank coordination and net-metering readiness.

Tenant AC planning

No central HVAC. Tenants install their own AC systems, but the architect must hide outdoor units in louvered side/back service zones.

Secure circulation

Bank public entry from front only. Café and gym visitors use a concealed lobby, lift and staircase from the rear/right side.

Bank security

Strong room, ATM, public/private zoning, secure staff access, CCTV, and controlled internal connection between ground and first floor.

04 / Required deliverables

Ask for a complete architectural package.

The architect should quote clear deliverables for approvals, technical execution, interior design and marketing material.

Architectural & technical drawings

✓Complete AutoCAD site plan with subdivision, roads, setbacks and orientation.
✓Basement feasibility plan, if parking/storage/services are possible and approvable.
✓Ground floor and first floor bank plans with secure internal access.
✓Second café floor, third gym floor and rooftop solar/service plan.
✓Front, right-side and back elevations respecting 39 ft frontage and 60 ft depth.
✓Sections showing floor heights, lift/stair core, façade depth, solar canopy and service shafts.

MEP, visuals & marketing assets

✓Electrical drawings: main panel, tenant sub-meters, generator, solar, earthing and DBs.
✓Plumbing and sanitary drainage drawings including washrooms, roof drainage and café/service drainage.
✓Fire safety drawings: exits, alarm, extinguishers, hose reel/hydrant, smoke detection and emergency lights.
✓AC-ready service design: outdoor unit locations, louver screens, sleeves, drains and isolator points.
✓Interior concepts for bank, café, gym, lobby, reception zones and washrooms.
✓High-end renders, walkthrough videos and a marketing prospectus for bank/tenant pitching.
05 / Meeting questions

Keep the conversation focused.

Use these questions one by one so the meeting reaches decisions instead of becoming a general discussion.

01
What are the exact bylaws for this plot?Confirm setbacks, ground coverage, height, parking, fire exit, basement permission, signage and solar canopy allowance.
02
Can the project legally support G+3 plus service roof?Confirm rooftop solar, stair head, lift machine room, water tank and service structures.
03
Can we create a secure two-floor bank?Bank customers should enter from front only while café/gym use separate rear/right-side access.
04
Where should the single lift and staircase core go?Save rentable area while keeping bank security intact and giving independent access to upper floors.
05
Is basement feasible or worth it?Discuss approval, waterproofing, parking, services, generator or storage use.
06
How will split AC outdoor units be concealed?Require a clean louvered service band on side/back so the elevation stays premium.
07
What insulation system is best for Hyderabad?Discuss wall thickness, roof insulation, shading devices, low-glass openings and façade materials.
08
What will be included in the architect's fee?Confirm drawings, renders, video, interior design, MEP coordination, BOQ, approvals support and timelines.
06 / Suggested workflow

Work in phases, not guesswork.

Ask the architect to move from feasibility to planning, then elevation, then technical drawings and finally marketing material.

Phase 01

Feasibility

Site verification, bylaws, basement possibility and G+3 / rooftop solar allowance.

Phase 02

Planning

Circulation, bank security, café/gym access, floor layouts, service zones and parking/drop-off.

Phase 03

Elevation

Low-glass façade, green strategy, solar canopy, signage zones and AC service concealment.

Phase 04

Technical + marketing

MEP, fire, structural coordination, interiors, renders, videos and prospectus.
